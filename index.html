Started in QA. Stayed for the full stack.

I'm a full stack engineer, mostly in TypeScript, from Damak, Nepal. I studied Computer Engineering (2019–2024) at Madan Bhandari Memorial Academy Nepal under Pokhara University.

My first jobs were in QA, which taught me how systems fall over. That shapes how I build across the stack: React interfaces that handle the unhappy paths, and NestJS, Express and Django services that are boring to operate and easy to reason about.

Away from the keyboard: algorithms puzzles, the global economy, anime, films, music and the occasional carrom match.
